# Product Vision

## Vision
Create the trusted interface through which professionals and organizations interact with Ghanaian law, then extend the infrastructure across African jurisdictions.

## Product Promise
**Find the law. Understand the law. Research the law. Work with the law.**

## Four Layers
1. **Legal Data** — cases, legislation, regulations, court rules and approved authoritative materials.
2. **Legal Intelligence** — search, retrieval, relationships, summaries, citation treatment and synthesis.
3. **Research Workspace** — saved authorities, notes, projects, reports and collaboration.
4. **Legal Workflow** — monitoring, private knowledge, document intelligence and enterprise integrations.

## Experience
The primary interface is **Ask the Law**. A user enters a research question and receives a source-grounded research result containing issues, authorities, relevant passages, similar cases, contrasting authorities and a research trail.

## Trust Principle
AI output is never a substitute for the underlying authority. Every important legal proposition should be traceable to source material.
