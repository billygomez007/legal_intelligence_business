# Target Users

## Primary Beachhead: Lawyers
Needs: faster research, better authority discovery, case analysis, citation checking, research reports and alerts.

## Law Firms
Needs: team workspaces, shared research, internal precedents, private knowledge, permissions and administration.

## Corporate Legal & Compliance
Needs: legal monitoring, regulatory change tracking, document analysis, enterprise search and internal knowledge.

## Law Students
Needs: case briefs, explanations, research training, study tools and affordable access.

## Researchers / Academics
Needs: historical case research, citation networks, legal trends and datasets.

## Public
Needs: plain-language explanations of public legal information and legal processes. Consumer experiences must preserve the distinction between legal information and individualized legal advice.

## Launch Priority
1. Lawyers
2. Law firms
3. Students
4. Corporate legal/compliance
5. Researchers/institutions
6. Broader public
