# Source Verification Standard

## Objective
A legal research answer must be inspectable from proposition to source.

## Requirements
Each material legal proposition should, where feasible, map to:
- document ID
- case/statute citation
- court/source type
- passage/page/paragraph locator
- source text
- source/version date

## Verification States
- Verified: source and passage support the proposition.
- Partially verified: source is relevant but proposition requires qualification.
- Unverified: system could not establish adequate support.
- Human reviewed: reviewed by authorized legal-data staff.

## Prohibited Behavior
- Inventing a case.
- Inventing a citation.
- Quoting text not present in the source.
- Treating AI summary as primary authority.
- Hiding lack of evidence.

## UI
Users must be able to open the authority from the AI answer. Source text and AI synthesis should be visually distinguishable.

## Corrections
Reported source errors should enter a tracked data-quality workflow with audit history.
