# Monetization

## Free
- Basic search
- Limited AI questions
- Basic case summaries
- Public legal education

## Professional
For individual lawyers:
- Advanced search
- Ask the Law
- Similar cases
- Citation intelligence
- Research workspace
- Alerts
- Research reports

## Law Firm
- Professional features
- Team seats
- Shared research
- Private knowledge base
- Administration
- Advanced security and audit capabilities

## Corporate / Enterprise
- Legal monitoring
- Private knowledge
- Document intelligence
- Team administration
- API/integrations
- Advanced security and contractual controls

## Institutional / API
Universities, approved technology partners and institutions can license access to structured legal intelligence subject to source rights.

## Pricing Strategy
Do not hard-code foreign-market assumptions. Conduct willingness-to-pay interviews and paid pilots in Ghana. Test monthly and annual pricing, per-seat pricing, firm packages and enterprise contracts.

## Revenue Principle
Do not depend on selling individual PDFs. Optimize for recurring subscriptions and enterprise value.
