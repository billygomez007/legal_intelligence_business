# Business Model

## Business Thesis
Legal information in Ghana is fragmented, difficult to search, inconsistently structured, and time-consuming to convert into usable legal research. The opportunity is not a simple case database; it is an **AI-powered legal intelligence platform** that structures legal information, connects authorities, and turns research into usable professional work product.

## Positioning
**AI-powered legal intelligence and research infrastructure for Ghana and Africa.**

## Value Proposition
The platform helps users move from a legal question to:
1. Relevant legislation
2. Relevant cases
3. Similar factual authorities
4. Supporting and distinguishing authorities
5. Current legal position
6. Source passages
7. Research notes
8. A structured research report

## Revenue Segments
- Individual lawyers: advanced research, analysis, citation intelligence, saved research, alerts.
- Law firms: collaboration, shared workspaces, private knowledge bases, administration.
- Corporate legal/compliance: legal monitoring, regulatory intelligence, document analysis, alerts.
- Universities/researchers: institutional research and structured datasets.
- API/data: approved structured legal-data and intelligence services.

## Strategic Moats
- Clean structured legal corpus
- Legal citation graph
- Legal knowledge graph
- Human-reviewed metadata
- Source-grounded AI workflows
- Private organizational knowledge
- Enterprise integrations
- Multi-jurisdiction infrastructure

## Business Model
Recurring SaaS subscriptions are the core model. Enterprise and API/data products expand ARPA. Public/student access supports acquisition and long-term professional conversion.

## Boundary
The initial product is legal information, research intelligence, and workflow—not a lawyer-referral marketplace or automated legal representation.
