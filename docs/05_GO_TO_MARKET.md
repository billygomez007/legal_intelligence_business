# Go-To-Market

## Phase 1: Lawyer-First
Recruit 50–100 design-partner lawyers in Accra, Kumasi and other relevant professional hubs.

### Message
**Spend less time searching. Spend more time practicing law.**

### Demo
Show: question → authorities → relevant passages → similar cases → research report.

## Lawyer Advisory Council
Include litigators, corporate lawyers, property lawyers, academics, researchers and younger practitioners. Use them to validate terminology, workflows and AI quality.

## Phase 2: Students
Partner with law schools, legal research societies and student groups. Use student access as a long-term professional acquisition channel.

## Phase 3: Corporate
Sell monitoring and legal intelligence to banks, fintechs, insurers, telecoms, real-estate businesses, energy companies, NGOs and large SMEs.

## Phase 4: Institutional/API
Universities, research organizations and approved technology partners.

## Phase 5: Geographic Expansion
Add jurisdictions one at a time with local corpus, hierarchy, citation rules, regulatory review and expert validation.

## GTM Metrics
- Design partners recruited
- Weekly active lawyers
- Search success
- Trial-to-paid conversion
- Sales cycle
- Seat expansion
- Retention
