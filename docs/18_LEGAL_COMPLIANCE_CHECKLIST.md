# Legal & Compliance Checklist

This is a planning checklist for review with qualified Ghanaian counsel.

## Source Rights
- Identify every legal-content source.
- Determine copyright/licensing status.
- Confirm permission for commercial reproduction/indexing.
- Confirm AI-processing rights.
- Track provenance and restrictions.
- Maintain a source-rights register.

## Court / Government Materials
- Confirm access terms.
- Confirm redistribution terms.
- Confirm licensing/permission needs.
- Confirm restrictions on bulk collection.

## Data Protection
- Determine controller/processor roles.
- Register where legally required.
- Publish privacy notice.
- Define lawful bases.
- Define retention periods.
- Provide data-subject rights workflows.
- Establish processor agreements.
- Implement breach-response process.

## Legal-Services Boundary
- Review whether features could constitute reserved legal practice or regulated referral activity.
- Avoid presenting AI as a licensed lawyer.
- Review consumer-facing language.
- Review any lawyer-directory or marketplace concept before implementation.

## AI
- Review professional-liability exposure.
- Review automated-decision implications.
- Avoid unsupported outcome probabilities.
- Maintain human verification paths.

## Contracts
- Terms of service
- Privacy policy
- Enterprise DPA
- Acceptable use policy
- API terms
- Data-license agreements
