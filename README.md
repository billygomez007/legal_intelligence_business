# AI-Powered Legal Intelligence Platform

AI-powered legal research and intelligence infrastructure for Ghana, designed to expand across Africa.

## Vision
Build a trusted legal intelligence operating system that transforms case law, legislation, regulations, court rules, and legal documents into searchable, explainable, source-grounded intelligence for lawyers, firms, businesses, students, researchers, and the public.

## Core Promise
**Find the law. Understand the law. Research the law. Work with the law.**

## Product Principles

1. **Source-grounded by default** — legal propositions should link back to primary or trusted sources.
2. **Never fabricate authority** — when authority cannot be verified, the product should say so.
3. **Research assistance, not automated legal representation** — maintain a clear product boundary.
4. **Data quality is a product feature** — provenance, document versioning, OCR confidence, and review status matter.
5. **Human-in-the-loop for high-impact legal metadata** — important classifications should be auditable and reviewable.
6. **Lawyer-first launch** — optimize the first commercial product for professional legal research.
7. **Ghana first, Africa-ready architecture** — jurisdiction must be a first-class system concept.
