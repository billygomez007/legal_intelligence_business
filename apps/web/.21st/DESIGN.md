# LexGhana Legal Intelligence design context

LexGhana is a professional legal intelligence and research platform for Ghanaian lawyers and firms.

## Visual identity

The product uses a dark institutional palette:

- Deep background: `#06101d`
- Elevated background: `#091728`
- Surface: `#0d1d31`
- Strong surface: `#11243b`
- Border: `#213956`
- Ghana gold: `#d3a62d`
- Gold hover: `#e4bc51`
- Primary text: `#f5f1e8`
- Secondary text: `#b4bfcc`
- Muted text: `#78889d`

Major legal and editorial headings use a restrained serif stack. Navigation, controls, metadata, filters and tables use the application sans-serif stack.

## Product feel

The interface should feel:

- authoritative
- premium
- institutional
- Ghana-first
- research-focused
- information-dense
- trustworthy

Avoid generic white SaaS layouts, neon AI styling, crypto aesthetics and decorative gradients.

## Application shell

Desktop uses a fixed navy sidebar with gold active navigation. Research pages use dark content surfaces and clear visual boundaries between source material, structured metadata, synthesis and user notes.

Mobile uses an accessible Radix dialog drawer.

## Trust

Synthetic records remain visibly marked as demonstration content.

Verification badges, source availability and provenance are interface states only and are not presented as production legal verification.

Primary source content, structured metadata, AI synthesis and user-authored notes must never visually blur together.
